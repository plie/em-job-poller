ABOUT GROUP PRACTICES AT HEADWAY

Thousands of group practices run their insurance business on Headway. They range from three-clinician practices to multi-state organizations with dedicated operations staff, and together they are one of the largest and fastest-growing parts of our business. But the product they use was built for the solo therapist. Owners and admins manage calendars, onboarding, session confirmations and payouts one clinician at a time, and many still split their caseload across several platforms.

We're changing that. Group Practices runs as a startup within Headway: one cross-functional team, with a single business owner, that owns how multi-provider practices find, join, operate and grow on Headway, across go-to-market, product and customer success. Supervisory billing is a core part of this. It lets pre-licensed clinicians see clients under a licensed supervisor, it's the capability groups ask us for most, and we're taking it from a handful of states to national scale.

ABOUT THIS ROLE

As Senior Engineering Manager for Group Practices, you'll lead the engineering organization that owns growth and experience for group practices end to end. That covers how a practice discovers and signs up for Headway, onboarding every clinician in the group, the tools owners and admins use every day to run their practice, and supervisory billing. You'll start with a small, high-trust team and grow it into multiple pods, hiring and developing the engineers, tech leads and future managers who staff them.

You'll work as a peer to the Group Practices product lead, who runs the business like a general manager, and alongside design, data, product marketing, sales, customer success, operations, legal and our payer team. Near-term work includes:

 - Give admins superpowers: a practice-wide control center, a unified calendar, bulk session adds and confirmations, finer-grained roles and permissions, and group onboarding that doesn't repeat for every provider

 - Sell to groups like a business: group-specific landing pages, lead forms, routing and funnel experiments

 - Scale supervisory billing nationally: a configurable state launch engine, supervisor reassignment, a smooth path from supervisee to licensed provider, and compliance and quality oversight built into the product

 - Rebuild the foundations: group practices began as a fork of our solo product. You'll set the technical direction for the data model, roles and permissions, and rate infrastructure that let every Headway team build for groups by default

Our interview process
