About New Verticals at Headway

Most people don't find a therapist by typing into a search bar. They ask their doctor, check their insurance card, or pick up the phone. New Verticals builds those front doors. When a doctor tells a patient "you should talk to someone," we want Headway to be the next step. When someone looks for care in their insurer's directory, we want them to find it and book it. When someone calls, we want them to get booked on that call.

These channels already carry a large and growing share of Headway's new patients, and most of what they could be is still unbuilt. We are early, and the team you lead will decide what this becomes.

About this role
