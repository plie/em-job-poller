Relay is a digital banking platform that gives self-made business owners the tools and know-how to be great with money—bringing clarity, confidence, and control to every dollar earned, so they can turn hard work into lasting success. We do this by replacing financial guesswork with real visibility, transforming cash flow from a constant source of stress into a clear signal owners can use to run stronger, more resilient businesses.



Relay's data engineering team has tripled in size, from 4 to 12, and is now splitting into two teams. Analytics Data Engineering works close to the business, building the models, pipelines and dashboards that teams use to run the company. Data Platform, the team you'll lead, owns the foundation underneath: infrastructure, cost, real-time data, observability, and the AI capabilities the rest of Relay builds on.



The team's north star is platform health. That means a fast, reliable, cost-efficient platform with strong observability, and partners across the company who can do their work without waiting on us.



What You'll Be Doing

 - Lead and grow a senior team of data engineers. With an experienced team in place, your role is to provide direction, challenge thinking, and help the team make decisions and move work forward.

 - Own Snowflake cost. In your first 90 days, you’ll develop and execute a plan to reduce Snowflake costs month over month, working closely with the team to identify and deliver savings.

 - Get data into stakeholders' hands faster. You'll define and drive Relay's approach to real-time data and platform enablement, including streaming (Kafka) and a Snowflake MCP server that opens our data to AI tooling.

 - Make platform health visible. Build observability so the team finds a problem before a stakeholder does.

 - Translate platform work into business outcomes. You’ll help the team prioritize upgrades, migrations and refactors by linking technical investments to business value, whether that’s driving revenue, improving efficiency or reducing risk.

 - Balance speed against technical debt, in both directions. Sometimes you'll persuade the team to ship with some debt because the business value is there. Sometimes you'll persuade the business to give the team more time. You'll know which is which.

 - Run the team's operating rhythm. That includes standups, sprint and roadmap planning, architectural decisions, code review, and the requests that come in from across Relay.



Who You Are

 - You have at least two years of experience managing engineers, including senior team members with strong technical perspectives and the expertise to back them up.

 - You have deep, hands-on production experience with Snowflake and dbt, including building data models.

 - You've owned a production data platform, and ideally you've led meaningful improvements to one, such as cutting cost, improving reliability or adding new capability.

 - You challenge decisions with curiosity, not authority. You ask why before you ask how long, and you can find a path that serves both the engineer's concern and the business deadline.

 - You lead with context. You explain the problem, the metric and the business case before the architecture.

 - You're highly fluent with AI tools, both in how you work and in how you'd guide a team building AI-enabled data capabilities.

Our Compensation Approach

We believe Relayers should feel rewarded for the impact they have on our mission and growth. Compensation follows impact. As impact increases, compensation grows, and we do not limit compensation changes to a once-a-year review cycle.

The annual salary range for this role is 207,000 CAD to 253,000 CAD.

Offers below this point reflect candidates we believe can grow into the full scope of the role with support and development.
