<div class="content-intro"><p><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Who We Are</strong></span></p>
<p>Addepar is a global data and AI platform empowering investment professionals to turn complex financial information into actionable intelligence. Addepar unifies portfolio, market and client data in a total portfolio view and delivers AI-powered insights within investment and client workflows. More than 1,400 firms in nearly 60 countries use Addepar to manage and advise on nearly $9 trillion in assets. Its open platform integrates with nearly 650 software, data and consulting partners to power end-to-end investment operations across firms of all sizes and complexity. Addepar supports clients worldwide with offices in New York City, Salt Lake City, London, Edinburgh, Pune, Dubai, Geneva, Singapore and São Paulo.&nbsp;</p></div><h4><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>The Role</strong></span></h4>
<p>We are currently seeking a software engineering manager to join the Alternatives Data Management (ADM) team. Our team is building AI-powered data products and workflow solutions that unlock transparency, efficiency, and insight in private market investments. You’ll lead a full-stack team of engineers and collaborate closely with Product and Design to deliver client-facing features that will set us apart from our competitors.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Applicants must have legal authorization to work in the country where this role is based on the first day of employment. Visa sponsorship is not available for this position.</p>
<h4><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>What You’ll Do</strong></span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Manage a small team of frontend and backend engineers that will grow as you do</li>
<li>Own technical design and implementation for some of your projects to set a good example for the ICs</li>
<li>Work with platform teams to deliver features with consistent capabilities</li>
<li>Drive the career growth of your direct reports</li>
<li>Stay up to date on industry trends, such as AI tooling, that can make the team more effective</li>
<li>Collaborate with ADM engineering and product leads in the US, UK and India to drive team-wide process improvements and best practices&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<h4><span style="text-decoration: underline;"><strong>Who you are</strong></span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience</li>
<li>Minimum 10+ years of software engineering experience</li>
<li>Minimum 2+ years managing software engineering teams</li>
<li>Full-stack, product engineering experience a big plus</li>
<li>React experience a plus</li>
<li>Strong experience with Python OR Java will be required</li>
<li>Experience with AWS will be highly preferred</li>
</ul>
