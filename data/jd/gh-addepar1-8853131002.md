Who We Are 

 Addepar is a global data and AI platform empowering investment professionals to turn complex financial information into actionable intelligence. Addepar unifies portfolio, market and client data in a total portfolio view and delivers AI-powered insights within investment and client workflows. More than 1,500 firms in 60 countries use Addepar to manage and advise on nearly $10 trillion in assets. Its open platform integrates with 650 software, data and consulting partners to power end-to-end investment operations across firms of all sizes and levels of complexity. Addepar supports clients worldwide with offices in New York City, Salt Lake City, São Paulo, London, Edinburgh, Geneva, Warsaw, Dubai, Pune and Singapore.

 The Role 

 Addepar is seeking a highly skilled and motivated Engineering Manager to join the Product Platform team. The Product Platform team is at the heart of powering Addepar’s core engineering capabilities. We play a pivotal role in weaving Addepar data, core utilities, and user experience frameworks into a resilient, shared foundation that enables product teams, partners, and clients to build secure, consistent, and data-driven experiences at scale. Working at the critical intersection of high-scale administration, governance, and developer velocity, our team drives core pillars including decoupled authorization, centralized audit tracking, and high-performance search infrastructure. Working with a healthy mix of modern distributed architecture and existing core systems, the team thrives on solving complex technical challenges to deliver high-impact solutions across the entire ecosystem.

 In this high-impact role, you won't just lead a group of engineers—you'll be the driving force behind scaling the Addepar Platform and architecting cross-cutting solutions that redefine how our products interact. Your responsibilities will include:

 Driving the technical direction for cross cutting products across Addepar.

 Collaborating with teams from various departments to successfully implement platform strategies.

 Building and managing a team of exceptional engineers.

 Ensuring flawless and timely execution of deliverables.

 Developing and maintaining relationships with partners across Addepar.
