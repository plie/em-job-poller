At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

 About the Engineering team 
The Engineering team builds systems that power Affirm’s mission. We take pride in our craft, building reliable, performant, and scalable solutions that touch every part of the business. Whether we’re enabling new ways to pay, ensuring our platforms can scale to the most demanding levels in the industry, or developing seamless servicing experiences, our focus is on solving complex problems through exceptional engineering.

 About the role 
We’re looking for an experienced Engineering Manager to run the Payment Instruments team, which owns how payment methods are created, maintained, monitored and kept compliant globally, from cards and ACH to Apple Pay and new methods in global markets. You’ll grow engineers, set clear technical direction, and deliver reliable, trusted payments that advance our mission of honest financial products.

 What you’ll do 

 You will lead and grow the engineers who build, maintain and monitor Affirm’s global payment methods, from cards and ACH to Apple Pay and new methods in new markets.

 You will set a clear technical roadmap that turns Consumer Payments goals into executable plans, and keep the team focused on the highest-impact priorities.

 You will hire, develop and retain engineers at every level, with clear career paths and a high bar for craft, reliability and impact.

 You will partner with product, design, risk, compliance and other engineering teams to ship new payment methods and expand into new markets with speed and quality.

 You will make sure payment methods stay healthy and compliant by championing standards for reliability, performance, security and regulatory requirements.

 You will guide the team in improving a long-standing codebase step by step, so that each project leaves the platform simpler and stronger.

 You will foster a culture of ownership and curiosity, where the team uses AI-assisted tools and measures its impact.

 What we look for 

 You bring the experience and mindset to lead a team that moves money for millions of people.

 You have 10+ years of software engineering experience, including 4+ years leading and growing engineering teams.

 You have a track record of shipping and scaling consumer-facing payments or fintech systems.

 You are experienced in building and developing teams, including ramping up new joiners, and you create clarity in ambiguous situations.

 You are a strong partner who can influence and align peers and stakeholders across product, design, risk and compliance.

 You have experience with compliance, regulatory or multi-market requirements, and you appreciate how they shape system design.

 You are adaptable to change and make sound technical trade-offs when priorities shift.

 You hold a degree in Computer Science, Engineering or equivalent practical experience.
