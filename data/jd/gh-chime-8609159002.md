<h2><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><strong>About the role</strong></span></h2>
<p><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Chime is looking for an engineering leader to help manage and guide one of the Cards engineering teams within the Spending, Saving &amp; Investing (SSI) organization. SSI is on a mission to make Chime the top choice for everyday spending by building innovative, reliable, and member-first financial tools. We focus on driving active spend and usage, increasing reliability and availability, and delivering seamless, trusted experiences across cards, credit, money movement, saving and investing, deals, and transaction intelligence. Our work empowers members to manage their money with confidence, spend smarter, and improve their long-term financial health.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">We're hiring Tech Lead Managers (TLM) for multiple squads in Cards. TLMs at Chime manage a team of 3 to 5 engineers: Setting the hiring bar, coaching and developing talent, and driving all formal people management activities. They own execution and delivery for the squad. They stay in the code, authoring and shipping features alongside their team.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">The base salary offered for this role and level of experience will begin at $199,000 and up to $275,000. Full-time employees are also eligible for a bonus, competitive equity package, and benefits. The actual base salary offered may be higher, depending on your location, skills, qualifications, and experience.</span></p>
<h2><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><strong>In this role, you can expect to</strong></span></h2>
<ul>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Manage and develop a squad of 4-5 engineers: Hiring, 1:1s, career coaching, managing for performance</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Own delivery for your squad end to end, from sprint planning and delivery commitments through service SLAs and operational health</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Write and ship code alongside your team, authoring pull requests that land in production</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Be accountable for the squad's technical direction, architecture, and quality bar, either via a tech lead on the squad, or by taking on the tech lead role yourself.</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Use AI coding agents and tooling to raise both your own leverage and your squad's, and establish the practices that make that durable for the team</span></li>

<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Identify technical debt and advocate for sustainable engineering, staying an active contributor to the codebase</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Ensure quality, performance, and operational health, including a healthy on-call and incident-response culture</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Build growth paths for the engineers on your squad towards the higher IC levels or a future TLM role</span></li>
<li style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;"><span style="font-family: helvetica, arial, sans-serif;">Contribute to broader SSI and Chime engineering initiatives as a member of the engineering leadership team</span></li>
</ul>
