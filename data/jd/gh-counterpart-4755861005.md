Financial Data Manager 

 Counterpart is the first Agentic Insurance™ platform, combining trusted insurance expertise with cutting-edge AI to deliver tailored management and professional liability solutions. By integrating Agentic Underwriting™, Agentic Broker Services™, Agentic Risk Mitigation™, Agentic Claims Management™, Agentic Coverage™, and Agentic Capacity™ into one powerful platform, Counterpart empowers brokers - and the businesses they serve - to do more with less risk. Backed by A rated carriers, Counterpart has been recognized by CB Insights as a 2025 “Top 50 Insurtech,” by Great Place to Work, and by Business Insurance as one of its 2025 “Best Places to Work.”

 We're seeking a Financial Data Manager to design and deliver the data workflows that power Counterpart's financial and accounting systems. You will report to Pricing & Analytics and serve three primary customers: the finance function, the analytics function, and broker services (billing). You will translate insurance accounting concepts — written and earned premium, unearned premium reserves, commissions, and cash application — into clear data requirements. You will then partner with engineering to build them. This is a design and project management role at the intersection of finance, data, and product.

 YOU WILL 

 Design data-driven workflows that feed Counterpart's financial and accounting systems and the downstream processes that depend on them.

 Translate finance, analytics, and billing requirements into clear data models, source-of-truth definitions, and technical specifications.

 Own the definitions behind core insurance accounting measures — written premium, earned premium, unearned premium, commissions, and ceded amounts — so every team reports the same number.

 Partner with engineering to implement these workflows, and manage the projects end to end from scoping through delivery and adoption. Enable resilient systems that can handle edge cases (even with easy manual override)

 Serve as the primary data partner to the finance function for the monthly and quarterly close, reconciliations, and audit support.

 Support broker services on billing and invoicing data, including installment schedules, cash application, and commission accuracy.

 Build controls and automated checks that catch premium, billing, and bordereau discrepancies before they reach carriers or auditors.

 Design and maintain the carrier and bordereau reporting pipelines, and make sure the outputs tie to the general ledger.

 Document data lineage, change tracking, business rules, and known limitations so finance and analytics users can self-serve with confidence.

 Prioritize a backlog of finance data requests, and set expectations with stakeholders on scope, sequence, and timing.

 Identify manual finance processes that data and automation can remove, and build the business case to fix them.

 YOU HAVE 

 Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, economics, information systems, or a related field.

 5+ years in financial data management, financial systems, insurance operations, or a comparable analytical role.

 A working knowledge of insurance accounting concepts, including written premium, earned premium, unearned premium reserves, and commissions.

 Practical SQL skills — you can query, join, and validate data on your own without waiting on an analyst.

 Experience writing requirements for engineering teams and managing delivery against them.

 Strong project management skills, and a track record of shipping cross-functional work with finance and technical partners.

 Clear written communication, and the ability to explain data decisions to accountants and engineers alike.

 Experience with data warehouses and business intelligence tools (Snowflake, Looker, dbt, or similar) is a plus.

 Insurance, insurtech, or fintech experience is highly desirable.
