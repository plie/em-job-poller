 Role Overview 

 As an Engineering Manager, you are a strong leader who helps build, guide, and inspire world-class teams of software engineers. You collaborate cross-functionally with Product Managers, Designers, and technical leads to guide your team in delivering high-impact, scalable product features. You foster an environment of continuous learning, technical excellence, and operational efficiency while supporting the career development and growth of your direct reports. You lead a team of 4-8 engineers, including senior ICs and partner with Staff+ engineers on technical direction, while taking ownership of engineering execution, helping your team navigate technical complexity, streamline delivery processes, and consistently ship high-quality software that solves critical problems for our customers.

 Our team is passionate, empathetic, hard working, and above all else focused on improving the lives of our service professionals (our Pros). Our success is their success.

 What you do each day: 

 Hire, mentor, and grow a team of talented software engineers

 Guide technical execution and team delivery from initial planning through release

 Partner with product and design leads to align technical execution with business priorities

 Cultivate engineering best practices, code quality standards, and efficient development workflows

 Participate in running agile planning and delivery processes that keep the team predictable and unblocked 

 Support individual career development through regular feedback, coaching, and goal setting

 Champion architectural improvements and technical debt resolution within your product area

 Foster a collaborative culture focused on autonomy, accountability, and continuous learning

 Qualifications: 

 2+ years of experience leading, managing, or mentoring software engineering teams

 6+ years of software engineering experience delivering scalable web or mobile applications

 Strong technical foundation across full-stack development, modern frameworks, and software architecture

 Proven experience driving team efficiency or output quality through the strategic use of AI tools and automation

 Demonstrated success in project management, agile methodologies, and cross-functional team delivery

 Excellent written and verbal communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences

 Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent work experience

 What will help you succeed: 

 Strong interpersonal skills with a commitment to empathetic leadership and team development

 High adaptability and comfort operating within fast-paced, evolving environments

 Uphold our values and principles

 A multi-dimensional background with a breadth of cross-functional interests or depth of specialized mastery

 Personal resilience to learn from scratch, and successfully thrive in ambiguity
