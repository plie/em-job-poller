<h2><strong>What’s the opportunity?</strong></h2>
<p>The AI Helpdesk is a major next step in the evolution of Intercom’s core product. We are shaping an AI-first Helpdesk that helps support teams work more efficiently, resolve more conversations, and deliver consistently excellent customer experiences.</p>
<p>This role sits in a high-impact product organisation where the product vision and technical direction are continuing to evolve. You’ll lead an engineering team working closely with Product, Design, Customer Support, and AI/ML partners to turn customer and operational problems into simple, powerful product experiences.</p>
<h2><strong>What will I be doing?</strong></h2>
<p>You will:</p>
<ul>
<li>Lead and grow a high-performing engineering team building AI-first Helpdesk capabilities.</li>
<li>Set clear goals and create the conditions for the team to deliver valuable, reliable product at a healthy pace.</li>
<li>Partner closely with Product and Design to shape strategy, roadmap, priorities, and customer outcomes.</li>
<li>Work with AI and ML partners where relevant, while keeping the focus on building excellent customer-facing product experiences.</li>
<li>Understand the customer, agent, and business problems behind the roadmap - not just the delivery process.</li>
<li>Bring technical judgment to architecture, quality, reliability, and long-term product health.</li>
<li>Remove impediments, create clarity, and help the team make good decisions with increasing autonomy.</li>
<li>Build an inclusive, collaborative team culture with high standards for ownership, communication, and execution.</li>
<li>Coach engineers and develop senior engineers into technical and organisational leaders.</li>
<li>Contribute to the wider AI Helpdesk strategy and help align engineering decisions across adjacent teams.</li>
<li>Explore how AI-assisted development can improve the team’s speed, quality, and effectiveness.</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>What are we looking for?</strong></h2>
<p>The most important thing is that you are an exceptional people leader who is personally action-oriented. We don’t want managers who are distant from the product, and we don’t want hands-on operators who neglect the leadership craft. You build strong teams and get involved directly when the situation requires it.</p>
<p>Beyond that, you bring:</p>
<ul>
<li>Experience managing software engineering teams building customer-facing products.</li>
<li>Strong product engineering instincts and an interest in customer problems, user experience, and measurable outcomes.</li>
<li>The ability to shape strategy, prioritise effectively, and create clarity in ambiguous environments.</li>
<li>Enough technical depth to engage credibly in architecture discussions, understand trade-offs, and unblock engineers when things get difficult.</li>
<li>A track record of delivering complex work predictably while balancing speed, quality, and reliability.</li>
<li>Experience partnering effectively with Product, Design, Data, Customer Support, and other senior stakeholders.</li>
<li>A strong record of coaching engineers, developing senior talent, and building healthy, inclusive teams.</li>
<li>Excellent written and verbal communication skills.</li>
<li>Curiosity about AI-native products and how AI-assisted engineering is changing the way teams build software.</li>
<li>A high degree of autonomy, sound judgment, and a bias toward action.</li>
</ul>
