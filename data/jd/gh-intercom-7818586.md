<h3><strong>What’s the opportunity?</strong></h3>
<p>We’re hiring an <strong>Engineering Manager</strong> for our <strong>AI Models Infrastructure Team</strong> in the <strong>AI Group</strong>. The AI Models Infrastructure team builds and operates the foundational infrastructure that empowers our teams to train and run Fin's own AI models.</p>
