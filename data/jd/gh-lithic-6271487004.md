Lithic is the modern card issuing and processing platform empowering ambitious financial companies to build the future of payments.

 Our infrastructure powers card programs for 100+ innovative clients, from fintechs reimagining credit and digital banking to platforms transforming disbursements and spend management. Companies like Mercury, Flex, and Novo rely on Lithic's developer-friendly APIs, direct network connections, and flawless reconciliation to launch and scale card programs in weeks, not years.

 We're building a future where access to better financial products materially improves people's lives, free from the constraints of 30-year-old mainframes and legacy processors. We're proud to be backed by world-class investors who share that vision, including Bessemer Venture Partners, Index Ventures, Spark Capital, Stripes, and Mastercard, along with many others. 

 We're a team of 170+ across 26 states and 7 countries, headquartered in New York City. 

 We are hiring an Engineering Manager to lead Lithic's Analytics team. The team owns the data that our sponsor banks, customers, and internal teams run on: the daily reporting suite our partner banks use to reconcile and oversee their card programs, the ledger and balance data behind Treasury, network reporting, and the analytics that drive product and business decisions. Much of this work ships to external parties on a schedule, so correctness and predictability matter as much as speed.

 This is a role for an experienced manager who is ready to lead from day one, not a stepping stone into management. It is also a player-coach role: we expect roughly 20% to 30% of your time to be hands-on, reviewing models and pull requests, unblocking the team, and shipping when it counts. The stack is dbt, Snowflake, Airflow, and Omni, with SQL and Python throughout.

 What You'll Do: 

 Lead and grow a team of six analytics engineers, owning performance, development, and overall team health

 Own the team's roadmap: scope it to real capacity, set dates the team can hit, and re-plan openly when priorities or estimates change

 Partner with Product, Treasury, Finance, Partnerships, and peer engineering managers to agree on requirements and dependencies before dates are committed

 Keep stakeholders and leadership informed with clear, complete status, including risks and slips, without being asked

 Ensure the accuracy and on-time delivery of external data products, including daily bank reporting, balance and reconciliation files, and quarterly network reporting

 Stay hands-on roughly 20% to 30% of the time: review dbt models and SQL, weigh in on data modeling and design, and contribute code

 Set data architecture direction for the team in partnership with the Director: what belongs in the warehouse vs an operational store, and what is fit for an external API vs analytical reporting

 Raise the bar on data quality and operations: testing, freshness and reconciliation checks, incident response, and documentation

 Give regular, written feedback and build clear growth paths for each person on the team

 Recruit, hire, and retain strong data talent

 What You'll Bring: 

 3+ years managing data, analytics engineering, or data engineering teams as a line manager, including coaching, written feedback, performance management, and hiring

 A track record of running a team through a full roadmap cycle: planning against capacity, delivering to committed dates, and adjusting the plan in the open

 Strong hands-on SQL and data modeling skills, with recent experience in a modern data stack (dbt, Snowflake or a similar warehouse, Airflow or a similar orchestrator)

 Experience designing data systems that serve external consumers, and the judgment to explain the trade-offs

 Experience owning data products with external consumers or contractual deadlines, where correctness is not negotiable

 Proven ability to work across Product, Finance, and Engineering and to get alignment from peers before committing your team

 Clear, direct communication, written and verbal, with engineers, executives, and non-technical partners

 Comfort staying close to the work while delegating effectively

 Experience with financial data (ledgers, reconciliation, payments, banking, or regulatory reporting) is strongly preferred

 An interest in fintech and payments

 The annual US salary range for this role is $169,000 - $265,000 plus equity. This salary range may be inclusive of several career levels at Lithic and will be narrowed during the interview process based on the candidate’s experience and qualifications. 

 This is a remote position. However, candidates must be located in the United States or Canada (Ontario or British Columbia). We do not offer visa sponsorship or assistance.
