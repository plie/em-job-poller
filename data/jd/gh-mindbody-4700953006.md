<p>You are an experienced engineer who has successfully transitioned into a people leader, passionate about building and operating reliable, scalable cloud platforms and developer infrastructure that empower engineering teams. You possess extensive experience in at least one aspect of developing a world-class cloud platform and can effectively manage a high-performing team throughout the entire process.</p>
<p>You’ll&nbsp;thrive in this role with experience in:</p>
<ul>
<li>3+ years of direct management experience with an&nbsp;engineering team of at least 6 members&nbsp;with skills across the stack</li>
<li>Hands-on experience building and operating production cloud platforms and Kubernetes/CNCF ecosystems in the public cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure).</li>
<li>Strong grounding in infrastructure-as-code and CI/CD (Terraform/Pulumi/CloudFormation, GitOps), with operational rigor across incident response, observability, networking, identity, compute, and storage at scale.</li>
<li>Proven ability to&nbsp;establish&nbsp;and articulate a vision, set goals, and execute effectively.</li>
</ul>
