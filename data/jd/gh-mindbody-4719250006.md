At Playlist, life's richest moments happen when people step away from screens to move, connect, explore, and play. With popular brands like Mindbody and ClassPass, Playlist empowers businesses and individuals, making it effortless for aspirations to become actions.  Mindbody equips wellness entrepreneurs with technology to support thriving businesses and create exceptional experiences. Innovation and curiosity drive our culture, connecting businesses and individuals through cutting-edge solutions. Join us if you're passionate about enhancing wellness through technology.

 The Role You’ll Play

 You’ll help keep assigned projects on track by aligning people, priorities, and resources to deliver key milestones. You’ll lead and develop a software engineering management team, strengthen coding and problem-solving capabilities, and help build a high-performing backend and core software organization for Mindbody. In this role, you’ll partner closely with design and business teams to bring useful, usable, and customer-centered product experiences to life

 Build and manage a talented backend development organization focused on delivering meaningful product features

 Lead, coach, and develop software engineering managers and technical team members

 Establish development metrics and regular processes for assessing performance and identifying opportunities for improvement

 Create consistent project management and software development processes that support high-quality delivery in rapid iterations

 Apply strong design, business, and technical judgment to accelerate releases while advancing long-term product goals

 Champion software development excellence, customer centricity, and continuous improvement across the organization

 Present and evangelize prototyped concepts to diverse audiences through both formal and informal presentations

 Facilitate cross-functional teams, contribute to short- and long-term planning, and help resolve challenges constructively

 Experience You Bring

 7+ years of hands-on experience as a web development engineer

 7+ years of experience managing backend development teams

 5+ years of experience managing and leading technical employees

 Experience developing products for global markets

 Experience working in a highly agile or Scrum environment

 Demonstrated ability to recruit, mentor, and develop strong software engineering teams

 Strong judgment and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across design, business, and technical teams

 A commitment to serving customers, evolving how work gets done, and creating useful, usable experiences

 Technology:

 We value adaptability and problem-solving over specific tools. Mindbody is primarily a .NET and C# engineering organization running on AWS. Depending on your team and area of focus, you might work with:

 Backend: .NET 8, ASP.NET Core, Python, Django, Ruby, and Rails

 Frontend and mobile: React, TypeScript, Next.js, Ember, and React Native

 Data: PostgreSQL on Aurora, SQL Server, Redis, and MongoDB

 Infrastructure and delivery: AWS, Kubernetes on EKS, Lambda, Pulumi, Docker, Helm, Flux, Azure DevOps, GitHub, LaunchDarkly, and New Relic
