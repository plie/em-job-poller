<p>The Credit Cards &amp; Banking team is part of Robinhood’s Money Engineering organization. This team builds the products and systems behind some of Robinhood’s most ambitious consumer experiences, including Robinhood Gold Credit Card, Banking, and the next generation of card and banking products.</p>
<p>This is a high-impact engineering leadership role at the intersection of product innovation, scale, and reliability. Our team owns the full customer experience end to end—from mobile and frontend surfaces to backend systems and the infrastructure that powers them. We work on products used by millions, in a space where execution quality, technical rigor, and customer trust matter deeply.</p>
<p>As an Engineering Manager, you’ll lead a team of engineers building category-defining credit card and banking experiences. You’ll partner closely with product, design, data, compliance, operations, and peer engineering teams to define the roadmap, make sound technical decisions, and deliver exceptional products at scale. You’ll also play a central role in hiring, coaching, and developing a high-performing team.</p>
<p>We’re looking for bold thinkers, sharp problem-solvers, and leaders who want to help build the future of money.</p>
<p><strong>This role is based in our Menlo Park, CA or New York, NY office(s), with in-person attendance expected 5 days per week.</strong></p>
<p>At Robinhood, we believe in the power of in-person work to accelerate progress, spark innovation, and strengthen community. Our office experience is intentional, energizing, and designed to fully support high-performing teams.</p>
