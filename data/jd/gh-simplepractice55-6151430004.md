 The Role 

 As the Senior Engineering Manager, you will lead and support SimplePractice engineers in designing and developing high quality software solutions for our products. You'll set technical direction, grow the engineers on your team, and partner closely with product, design, and other stakeholders to turn requirements into software our customers rely on every day. This is a builder's role: expect to be in a design review, a customer conversation, and your team's code in the same week. Your role will focus on both building things well and also building the right things. 

 Responsibilities 

 Lead a squad of engineers, fostering collaboration and supporting their professional growth. 

 Set the technical direction for your squad and translate it into a clear, sequenced plan the team can execute. 

 Own architectural decisions across the full stack, from data model and APIs to the front-end. 

 Partner with product and design to shape the roadmap, not just deliver against it. Help the team decide what to build, then hold a high bar on how. 

 Provide mentorship to engineers across a variety of backgrounds and experiences. 

 Set clear performance expectations and provide direct, timely and actionable feedback. 

 Promote engineering best practices within your squad and across the entire engineering team. 

 See around the corner to anticipate technical and resourcing challenges. 

 Ensuring collaboration in a remote / hybrid work environment across cross-functional teams.
