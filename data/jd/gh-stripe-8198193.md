<h2><strong>Who we are</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>About Stripe</strong></h3>
<p>Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career.</p>
<h3><strong>About the team</strong></h3>
<p>The Billing Collections team builds the systems that turn invoices into successful, dependable revenue outcomes. The team develops the foundational capabilities that help businesses collect payments across diverse customer relationships, payment methods, and markets, while adapting intelligently when collection does not go as planned.</p>
<h2><strong>What you'll do</strong></h2>
<p>As the engineering manager for Billing Collections, you’ll own the operational and technical direction of the platforms that help Stripe users collect revenue reliably. You’ll lead the team that builds flexible, intelligent collection experiences that improve payment success, support customers through changing circumstances, and help businesses reduce unpaid balances and accelerate cash flow. You’ll shape the team as it expands to support increasingly sophisticated business models and global payment needs while working backwards from internal and external customers.</p>
<h3><strong>Responsibilities</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Lead a team responsible for the core architecture needed to enable billing collection</li>
<li>Own the technical strategy and roadmap for billing collection</li>
<li>Drive operational excellence across billing infrastructure systems</li>
<li>Hire and develop engineers as the team expands into new scope</li>
<li>Partner with stakeholders across organizations to deliver billing solutions</li>
<li>Work backwards from internal and external customer needs to define priorities and scope</li>
<li>Define and uphold engineering standards across a team owning business-critical billing infrastructure</li>
</ul>
