Who we are

 About Stripe

 Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.

 About the team

 The Stream Analytics team owns Stripe’s real-time online analytical processing (OLAP) solution. The platform serves low-latency analytics over large volumes of financial data, both to merchants through Stripe Dashboard products and APIs, and to internal teams through Stripe’s data products. The system is business-critical: its performance, data freshness, and reliability directly affect millions of businesses. The team tackles hard problems at a scale few companies reach, including real-time ingestion, query performance, multi-tenancy, cost efficiency, and onboarding a growing set of use cases. We value high ownership, technical rigor, and sustainable operations, and we move quickly while staying deliberate about changes that affect production.
