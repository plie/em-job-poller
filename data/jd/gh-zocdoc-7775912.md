<div class="content-intro"><p><strong>Our Mission<br></strong></p>
<p>You call. You wait. You call again. In every other part of your life, you book in seconds. In healthcare, you’re blocked.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p>We’re here to give power to the patient.&nbsp;</p>
<p>For nearly 20 years, we’ve built the leading healthcare marketplace - helping tens of millions of people find and book the care they need. Now, we’re going further: building our infrastructure beyond Zocdoc’s marketplace to<strong> </strong>power access to care wherever patients search, from provider websites and insurance directories to search engines, AI platforms, and more.</p>
<p>Healthcare still lacks something every other major consumer industry takes for granted: a seamless way to go from <em>seeking</em> to <em>getting</em>. We don’t want to own the front door to care; there isn't one. We want to make sure all of those doors open when patients are knocking.</p>
<p>Fixing healthcare starts with fixing access to it. And we're still just getting started.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p></div><p><strong>Your Impact on our Mission</strong></p>
<p>Zocdoc's most important asset is our people. As an Engineering Manager of our Frontend Platform &amp; Mobile team, you'll play a central role in defining how millions of patients and providers experience Zocdoc — starting from the foundation. You'll lead the engineering effort behind our accessible React component library and design system: the shared frontend infrastructure that every product team at Zocdoc depends on to ship fast, inclusive, and polished experiences across web and mobile. You'll partner closely with UX, Product, and Engineering leaders to drive adoption, accelerate frontend and mobile development organization-wide, and establish accessibility as a first-class engineering discipline. Your leadership will improve the speed and quality of how we build — and ultimately, the experience of every patient who relies on Zocdoc to find care.</p>
<p><strong>You'll enjoy this role if you are…</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Passionate about frontend development — React, design systems, accessibility, and mobile — and energized by building infrastructure that multiplies the output of every other engineering team</li>
<li>The kind of engineering leader who loves building platforms that others build on, and finds deep satisfaction in driving adoption across an organization</li>
<li>Committed to making accessibility a standard, not an afterthought, and excited to establish the processes that raise the bar company-wide</li>
<li>Drawn to building inclusive, psychologically safe teams where engineers grow, belong, and do their best work</li>
<li>A data-driven thinker who brings evidence to decisions and holds a high quality bar without sacrificing speed</li>
<li>Serious about your work, but not about yourself. Let's face it — the way things are going, you kind of need a sense of humor</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Your day to day is…</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Leading, managing, and growing a high-performing team of frontend engineers focused on design systems, accessible component libraries, and frontend platform tooling</li>
<li>Owning the strategy and roadmap for Zocdoc's design system and accessible React component library, ensuring it is the preferred foundation for feature development across all product teams</li>
<li>Driving accessibility across Zocdoc's web and mobile surfaces — establishing planning, build, and QA processes so accessible product development becomes a consistent, measurable standard</li>
<li>Partnering with UX, Product, and Engineering to build and evolve a scalable, accessible, mobile-responsive component library that works seamlessly across web and native mobile platforms</li>
<li>Identifying and delivering platform improvements that meaningfully speed up frontend and mobile development across the engineering organization</li>
<li>Partnering with Product, UX, and Analytics to research and prioritize roadmaps and set a clear technical direction</li>
<li>Mentoring and coaching engineers at all levels to deliver outstanding work in React.js and related frontend and mobile technologies</li>
<li>Working with cutting edge GenAI tools and technology to accelerate development velocity, improve engineering workflows, and raise the bar on what the team can ship</li>
<li>Setting technical strategy with a focus on scalability, developer experience, and ease of contribution across teams</li>
<li>Contributing to Engineering Culture at Zocdoc — helping shape career ladders, supporting cross-team events, interviewing, and building a more inclusive technology workforce</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>You'll be successful in this role if you have…</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Proven leadership experience shared UI infrastructure as a product: ship design system as a reliable cross-platform foundation, drive migration and adoption across web and mobile teams, and institutionalize accessibility, instrumentation, and quality as defaults.</li>
<li>The right person combines strong frontend platform judgment and cross-functional influence, and enough mobile fluency to unify web/iOS/Android direction without making any one client platform the center of gravity.</li>
<li>5+ years of experience building and shipping software, with a strong foundation in frontend technologies (React, TypeScript, JavaScript) and demonstrated experience in mobile development ( iOS or Android)</li>
<li>2+ years of experience leading, growing, and inspiring engineering teams</li>
<li>Experience building or significantly contributing to a design system or shared component library used across multiple product teams</li>
<li>A history of building polished, well-tested, accessible consumer products on web and mobile</li>
<li>The ability to integrate generative AI tools into daily workflows to automate tasks, foster innovation, and maximize productivity</li>
<li>Superb communication skills — you keep stakeholders aligned, manage competing priorities transparently, and build consensus with engineers at all levels</li>
<li>The mentality of an entrepreneur/owner and a strong bias to action</li>
<li>Humility. You believe in treating all people with dignity and respect, regardless of title or tenure, and you approach tough conversations with empathy</li>
<li>A Bachelor's degree in CS, Math, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience)</li>
</ul>
