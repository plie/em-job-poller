About Huckleberry
 
At Huckleberry, our purpose is simple yet powerful: to build life-changing products and experiences that help all families unlock everyday magic.
 
Today, more than 6.5 million families trust Huckleberry and our app to be their partner through parenthood. Real pediatric expertise works hand-in-hand with data science, AI, and behavioral science to provide support behind-the-scenes that guides every parent through their unique journey. 
 
We believe every family should feel taken care of—which is why we’re growing quickly and expanding our product offerings to help every family thrive.


About the Role
 
As the Engineering Manager, you will oversee the core products that made us the number 1 recommendation to new parents! You'll be dedicated to nurturing our cornerstone offerings, making sure they stay reliable and high-quality so we can continue to scale alongside the community we serve. You will bridge the gap between high-level business objectives and technical execution, transforming our core product strategy into a clear, actionable roadmap.  You will lead and mentor a high-performing team of engineers, fostering their professional growth and alignment, as well as working cross-functionally with all teams at Huckleberry that help us execute on our mission of helping every family thrive.
